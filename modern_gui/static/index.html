Shape the training recipe

MiniMax H3 quality protection — recommended minimum sigma 0.15

Important H3 controls. Dynamic Sigma is enabled by the Proven Quality preset; its Minimum Sigma field defaults to 0.15 and controls when the extra preservation pass is skipped.

Estimate the final adapter size from model tensor shapes.
Weight noise and structural depth

Experimental controls for overfitting and image structure. Depth can substantially increase training time and VRAM use.

Differential Output Preservation (DoP)

A separate preservation objective that teaches what should remain unchanged while the LoRA learns your trigger. It needs carefully chosen trigger and class words and adds training cost.

Krea 2 depth VAE and secondary GPU

Krea can decode the predicted image on another visible CUDA GPU while the DiT and Depth Anything remain on the training GPU. This is experimental; start with a short run and verify the device mapping in the startup log.

MiniMax depth compute and multi-GPU memory

Depth compares the current LoRA prediction with the training image's structure. The target is cached, but the current prediction must be decoded during every depth step. The secondary VAE needs at least 16 GB VRAM for its backward pass; its ~5 GB weight size alone is not enough to judge fit.
